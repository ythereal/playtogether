Rival Hospital 3D

Two hospitals, one town. Patients pick the hospital with the better reputation and fairer prices.

Getting started

  • Tap the dashed outline by the entrance to buy a reception desk, then hire a receptionist. Without both, no patients come.
  • Build a room the way Theme Hospital does: drag a blueprint on the floor (each room has a minimum size), tap a wall for the door, add windows if you like, wait while the site clears and the walls go up, then place the furniture one piece at a time (Rotate turns it, or right-click; “Place for me” does it all). You pay for the furniture as it arrives and for the room itself when you finish. Rooms can't cut off any part of the hospital. Every patient starts at a GP's Office. If the GP isn't sure, they visit diagnosis rooms (General Diagnosis, Ward, Cardiogram…), each adding to their diagnosis %, then return to a GP. The higher the %, the better their chance of surviving the cure.
  • Cures happen in the Pharmacy (drugs, with an effectiveness %), Psychiatric room (needs a psychiatrist), single-disease clinics, and the Operating Theatre (two surgeons; patients stay in a Ward first).
  • You have a few minutes to build before patients arrive. Press Open the doors to start early.

Staff

  • Doctors are Juniors (skill below 250), Doctors (250–799) or Consultants (800+), and can hold Researcher, Psychiatrist and Surgeon qualifications. Both hospitals hire from the same pool, which refreshes on the 1st of every month.
  • Staff get tired. Build a Staff Room so they can rest (Policy sets when). Pay at least the fair wage or morale drops every month; unhappy staff ask for a raise and walk out a month later if you ignore them. A bonus is a cheap fix.
  • A Training Room with a consultant teaches students skill and copies the teacher's qualifications. Handymen clean up, water plants and repair machines.

Machines, research, money

  • Every machine has a Strength. Each use wears it; past its strength it smokes and can explode, killing everyone inside and wrecking the room for good. Handymen repair it; Replace restores it. Fire extinguishers lower the odds.
  • The Research Dept. (researcher doctors) splits points across Cure and Diagnosis equipment (new rooms), Drugs, Improvements (machine strength, then cheaper rooms) and Specialisation.
  • The Casebook sets each charge as a % of a recommended price that grows with reputation. Overcharging drives patients to your rival; some cured patients refuse to pay. Insured patients pay at month end. The bank lends in $5,000 steps.
  • Corridor objects: benches, plants, radiators (warmth: heat level is in Policy), fire extinguishers and drinks machines. Patients need toilets, or they wet themselves.
  • Tap a room to edit it (it empties first; then move, buy or sell furniture, or go back a step to resize or move it, all free) or delete it (half the room's price and all the furniture back). Windows, extra space and nearby extinguishers, bookcases, skeletons and bins make staff a little happier each day.

Events

  • Faxes pop up at the bottom left: emergencies, VIP visits, raise demands, patients you need to decide about, epidemics (declare it, or cover it up: tap each patient with a red 🦠 over their head so a nurse vaccinates them (it turns into a blue 💉), then cure them before the inspector arrives). Earthquakes wear out every machine. Litter brings rats: tap to shoot them.

Controls

Move the mouse to the edge of the screen (or drag, scroll or use the arrow keys) to look round the hospital, F to go back to the front doors, V for the flat aerial view of the whole hospital, Shift+F or ⛶ for full screen, B to build, R or right-click to rotate furniture. F1 statement · F2 casebook · F3 policy · F4 research · F6 staff · F7 bank · F8 status · Tab switches hospital · Shift+A hides the advisor line.
