Rival Hospital 3D

Two hospitals, one town. The patients go wherever the reputation is higher.

How it works

  • Tap an empty lot to build a room. Every patient sees a GP's Office first to be diagnosed, then needs the room that cures their disease. No room for it? They're sent home and your reputation dips.
  • Rooms need staff: Staff hires doctors and nurses. Skilled staff treat faster and lose fewer patients, but cost more each month.
  • Patients queue in the corridor. Keep queues short or they storm off angry. A crowded reception turns people away.
  • Cures earn the fee and reputation. Deaths cost a lot of reputation. Salaries are paid at the end of each month.
  • Tap a room to see who's working there, or to demolish it (half refund). Ten lots is all you get, so choose your specialities.
  • Decorate the corridor with plants, benches and radiators: comfort makes queues more patient. A drinks machine earns a little cash, but the cans end up on the floor.
  • Sick patients sometimes throw up, and mess spreads to anyone nearby. Hire a handyman to mop up, or the monthly inspection costs you reputation.
