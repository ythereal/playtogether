Kitchen Rush

Co-op. Three minutes. An all-vegetarian menu: serve as many orders as you can, together.

The menu

Stations

How it works

  • Grab ingredients from the crates (left), chop them on the boards (right).
  • Potatoes become tikkis on the board, then fry in the pan; soup boils in the pot. Take them off before the red bar fills or they burn. Burnt food goes in the bin.
  • Plates are at the bottom. Put food on a plate, then deliver it at the window (top left) before the order's timer runs out.
  • Fast deliveries earn tips, and every delivery in a row multiplies them (up to ×4). A lost order breaks the combo.
  • Split the work: one chef chops and passes food over the island, the other cooks and plates.
